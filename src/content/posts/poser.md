**[Open the composer](/poser/)**: the whole thing, on its own page.

Type `1 5 6 4` and you have a progression. The numbers are scale degrees, and
the key and the mode decide which chords they turn out to be, so dragging the
mode from Ionian to Aeolian re-skins the same four numbers while the loop keeps
playing.

Under the chords sit the lanes: a kit, a bass, a pad, a lead. The bass and pad
patterns store chord tones rather than notes, so they follow whatever is typed
above them. Sections stay linked until you bake one, which is how a chorus gets
lifted a tone without the verse noticing.

Space loops the section. The backquote key turns the keyboard into an
instrument, and record keeps every pass of a lead so a bad one can be deleted.
Songs are saved in the browser and leave it as a file, as MIDI, or as a WAV.
